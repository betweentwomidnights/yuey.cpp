# Prompted rank-32 Koan AR/NAR experiment

The user authorized a rank-32 experiment after the
[Billie adapter inspection](sv-billie-review-2026-10-25.md), and accepts a learned
trigger and descriptive style prompts. The goal remains Koan-like instrumentation
on new musical content, with chords/melody and usable fidelity preserved. A neutral
caption is an optional diagnostic. It is not a requirement for this prompted workflow.

The previous [two-track AR pilot](koan-ar-pilot-2026-10-25.md) step 32 remains an
immutable listening reference. The user heard its Koan palette and retained chords,
with degradation; step 128 lost considerable melody. More rank and updates alone
do not establish improvement. The published Billie step 950 is a budget comparison,
not evidence that our instrumental data needs the same training duration.

## Data and conditioning

There are 18 unique 256-frame windows from 12 training tracks: 184.32 seconds of
audio. Six different tracks provide six held-out windows. This is broader track
coverage, still a small fraction of the supplied corpus. Training-window selection
requires at least eight transcribed note payloads, three distinct pitches and 25%
estimated note-time coverage. Those checks filter sparse transcriptions; they do
not prove pitch accuracy or synchronization. Held-out tracks keep their original
split and are never promoted into training.

All real windows use the existing MotherSuperior/MERT semantic labels and clean
VAE posterior means from the original audio. The user authorized proceeding despite
audible semantic reconstruction degradation. That decision is not a target-quality
pass. The semantic tokenizer, VAE and planner are not trained here.

The exact training/inference style caption is:

> koan_palette. Instrumental glitch hop and electronica, intricate syncopated
> breakbeats, detailed bass sound design, layered synthesizers and evolving textures.

The alias uses ordinary tokenizer tokens; no embedding is added. Each real AR
window has scored and score-dropped views, retaining instrumental instructions
and boundary tags. Two neutral generated sequences provide replay training; a
third generated sequence supplies evaluation-only retention. No Koan recording
is supplied as an inference style reference. The user's source WAV is excluded
as an exact training file; its underlying composition provenance is unknown.

## Staged training

AR and NAR each adapt all seven independent projections in all 28 layers at rank
32: 34,865,152 parameters per expert, 69,730,304 together. The fixed instrumental
AR companion is retained. The native acoustic expert is the starting decoder;
the v9 NAR adapter is not stacked into this experiment.

AR uses learning rate 2e-5, weight decay .01, gradient clip 1, shuffled balanced
epochs, semantic cross-entropy and teacher KL weight .2. The common model plus
instrumental companion supplies the teacher distribution before updates. It is
recomputed before resume. Held-out/retention losses remain unregularized semantic
cross-entropy. There are at most 950 updates, checkpoints/evaluations every 50,
and stopping after five non-improving scheduled evaluations once step 200 is
reached. The selected checkpoint minimizes saved held-out semantic loss.

NAR freezes the selected AR adapter. Caches are rebuilt from exact prompted
prefixes and real semantic codes with that complete AR stack. Clean VAE posterior
means supply acoustic targets. Two neutral generated examples provide replay;
their targets come from the native Q4 decoder conditioned on the selected AR,
and are explicitly distinguished from real-audio VAE means. NAR uses rank 32,
learning rate 3e-5, weight decay .01, clip 1, shuffled epochs and random flow noise
levels in [.05,.95]. Held-out flow MSE averages fixed-noise evaluations at .2/.5/.8.
The budget is 950 updates, evaluations every 100 (and the final step), saving
every 50. Three non-improving evaluations after step 300 stop the run. The common
NAR remains an explicit candidate if calibration never improves held-out loss.

Both stages use frozen F16 weight storage with F32 projection arithmetic,
checkpointed full attention and host AdamW. NAR does not backpropagate into AR.
The toolkit implementation inspected in the Billie review also detaches its
AR cache for acoustic training; a new differentiable KV interface is unnecessary
for this staged experiment. GGML remains at b77ae790e628270af46a8af1eca38441c23f1252.

## Correctness and listening decisions

Independent PyTorch implementations check AR teacher probabilities, KL gradients,
full-window forward/backward and first-update AdamW. NAR gradients are checked
before and after updates. Shuffled sampling and training resume are checked for
byte-identical optimizer state. The rank-32 full-window AR resource probe reached
5,646 MiB observed device memory. Resource observations are not size guarantees.

Listening compares the common model, previous rank-8 step 32, selected rank-32 AR,
and selected AR plus calibrated NAR under identical style prompts. Cases include
the full supplied piano/orchestral recording at two seeds, an authored score,
and a common-model generated plan. Planning remains isolated from learned AR
weights. Every primary comparison is rebuilt through the same decoder renderer;
the AR-only/NAR comparison shares exact semantic tokens. Additional 32/100-step
midpoint controls use fixed prefix, tokens and noise.

An additional temperature-.9 diagnostic compares fresh semantic sampling
against temperature 1.0 with both decoder renders held at 100 midpoint steps.
The primary comparisons retain temperature 1.0. Early NAR step 100 is also
retained as a listening control if a later checkpoint is selected by flow MSE.

Semantic loss, flow loss and token differences are numerical evidence. Perceived
Koan palette, melody preservation and fidelity require separate listening ratings.
Completed numerical results and selected checkpoints are tabulated below.

The first full-source seed-831001 AR-only preview has received user feedback:
rank-32 step 150 has a **better balance of palette and melody** than previous
rank-8 step 32 under the identical Koan caption. This is a preference on that
presented case, not a fidelity pass for NAR calibration or an all-mode verdict.
The follow-up changes rank, data coverage, captions and regularization together;
it does not isolate rank as the cause of that preference.

On the subsequent matched source comparison, the user rated AR-only step 150
versus AR-150/NAR-400 as **about the same**. The lower acoustic loss therefore
does not establish an audible NAR benefit on that case. AR-only remains the
user-preferred AR upgrade and the reference for additional acoustic tests;
the combined adapter is an experimental alternative.

After broader listening, the user reports the selected NAR fresh-score output
has the highest quality and strongest DnB character. The AR-only and AR/NAR
versions have essentially the same musical output, with NAR versions sounding
higher quality. This subsequent feedback favors the combined adapter for
interactive exploration. The user explicitly withholds a success verdict until
they can experiment in the web UI; the earlier single-source tie remains part
of the record.

## Local inference with the exports

Keep the common instrumental companion in both stages. For a new score, pass
the score directly to the second command. For standard generation, create the
common-model plan first. The user's input recording is transcribed to a score
before this flow; it supplies musical content rather than a Koan style recording.

```powershell
$bin = "build-ggml027-cuda/bin/Release/yue2-generate.exe"
$common = "models/yue2-instrumental-cot-full-v1.0-F16-LoRA.gguf"
$koan = "models/koan-yue2-r32-ar150-nar400-F32-LoRA.gguf"
# Comparison without NAR calibration:
# $koan = "models/koan-yue2-r32-ar150-F32-LoRA.gguf"
$style = "koan_palette. Instrumental glitch hop and electronica, intricate syncopated breakbeats, detailed bass sound design, layered synthesizers and evolving textures."
$runtime = @("--model", "models/YuE2-3B-GGUF/yue2-3.6B-v1.0-Q4_K_M.gguf",
             "--vae", "models/YuE2-3B-GGUF/yue2-vae-v1.0-F16.gguf",
             "--tokenizer", "models/YuE2-3B-GGUF/sidecars/yue2-qwen.tiktoken",
             "--device", "cuda", "--instrumental", "--lora", $common)
& $bin @runtime --prompt $style --plan-only --bars 8 --score-output new-plan.abc
& $bin @runtime --prompt $style --abc new-plan.abc --lora $koan --out koan-output.wav
```

The listening suite uses fixed durations and seeds for comparisons. These
commands use the ordinary score-following generation pipeline. Full-song
stability and automatic stopping with this adapter remain separate checks.

## Interactive web UI

The local UI at `http://127.0.0.1:8007` now discovers the combined export and
the AR-only comparison. The shared style panel exposes strength, the taught
prompt, temperature, decoder steps and an optional audio ceiling. Style weights
apply after common-model planning for generation, remix and edited-score
rendering. Real-audio-token continuation remains on its separate matching
decoder; style experiments use score continuation.

Real HTTP checks confirm identical plans with/without the style selection,
bit-identical common/zero-strength WAVs, changed audio at strength 1, and working
generation and audio-to-score remix routes. Browser checks cover selection,
settings persistence and actual generation. All 18 application CTests and 23
Python tests pass after the integration.

A normal eight-bar, 170-BPM UI generation produced 41.3 seconds for an 11.3-second
score and exhausted the existing generated-score safety allowance. This does
not isolate LoRA as the cause. A second UI generation with a 15-second ceiling
completed at 15 seconds. Automatic endings remain an evaluation item; the ceiling
is explicit and may cut a phrase. Use 0 to restore automatic stopping.
